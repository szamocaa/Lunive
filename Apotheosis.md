# Project: Lunive (The Lore)
The Game's Lore is around the Character, you start the game with, It is "Vael".

 # - Chapter 1 (Where did it went wrong?)
(1538) Vael is an ordinary young man, who always lived in Harmony. He was always chasing dreams,
which were mostly things,
that you could hold, grab, touch, or use. (It was never something complex)
But one day, it all went away...
His eyes were mesmerized when He saw a young lady at Whispering Woods.
Her name was "Emma".
She was younger than Vael, had beautiful brown long hair.
and dressed up looking like shes from a wealthy family.
Vael approached the young lady, and said :

- You're as beautiful, as a boundless,
undiscovered sea whose deepest trenches hide pearls enough to buy a kingdom.

Emma just smiled. She didn't really know, what to say.
Vael felt disappointed about his words:

- Was I too... weird?
- Does she even understood my metaphor?
- Maybe she didn't, never mind...
