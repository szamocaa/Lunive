4.0.0: Lunive is out of beta. The Beta labels are gone: this is the official game. New in the Narukami event: Shogun's Wrath, a 2D pixel RPG in the EVENTS section. You are Amane, the girl who fell with the last cherry petals, in Narukami, an old Japanese city that is forgetting itself. Walk with WASD, talk with E, and fight turn by turn like the old pocket-monster games. Win a sword from Tetsu the blacksmith early on, relight the Memory Lanterns, mend the Great Bridge, cross into the Old Quarter, and gather the three seals of the Mountain Gate. There are shops, a bag, gear, a quest log, a map, memories, and levels up to 10 (HP and ATK grow every level). At level 10 the gate opens, and the Shogun waits at the top of Narukami Mountain, where the cherry blossoms were born. She takes you to the Realm That Faded Away, shows her true form and draws the Oblivious Blade; if it hits you, every memory you made flashes past. The fight is hard but fair, and in the end you unlock the Blazing Masamune. Help Vael the fisherman find Emma's lost letter and he gives you infinite HP and ATK. About 50 minutes, saved as you go; finish it for the title "The Petal That Fell" and 200 rubies. Also: hide the left panels (Missions, Online, Mail, Artifacts) with the arrow and slide them back; the banner picture has a plain white outline when you have no frame; the fullscreen button lives only in Settings; the Limited pets look cleaner; your equipped skin shows everywhere; and the coin and ruby counters no longer stack on top of each other.
3.40.0: Teams of three. A party leader can invite two friends now (the code or the INVITE button), and Sanatorium Online takes all three of you: each of you wakes in your own bed (a third bed in 107), you see and hear the other two walk the halls, each with a name over their head and a line in the tracker, and friendly fire hits only the one you shot. If one leaves, the other two carry on. In the lobby you stand in the middle with a teammate on each side. 1v1, 2v2 and Hide & Seek stay two-player.
